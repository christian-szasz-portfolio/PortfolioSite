This website uses no cookies and employs no third-party analytics or advertising services. No third-party scripts, fonts or tracking technologies are loaded. A small amount of information is stored locally on your device to remember your preferences, and, only with your consent, an anonymous record of visits and of how the website is used is maintained. This notice explains both.

Information stored on your device

To remember your preferences, the website stores a small amount of information locally within your browser. This is limited to your selected display theme, your response to the consent request, and the date of your most recent recorded visit. This information remains on your device until you clear this website's site data, is not transmitted with any request, and cannot be used to identify or track you.

Visit statistics

With your consent, the website keeps an anonymous visit count, a country breakdown and a record of how it is used, none of it identifying. No advertising identifiers or visitor profiles are created. Retention periods and the legal basis are set out in the privacy policy. If you decline, nothing is recorded.

Visits by country

Country information is derived from the IP-to-country database provided by DB-IP, used under the Creative Commons Attribution licence.

Consent

On your first visit, a notice requests your consent to the visit statistics described above. If you accept, they operate as set out here; if you decline, nothing is recorded and your network address is not processed. Your choice is retained so that the request is not repeated.

Withdrawing consent and removing information

You may withdraw your consent and remove all information held on your device at any time by clearing this website's site data through your browser settings. Browsing in a private window will similarly discard it once the window is closed.

External links

This website contains links to external services, including GitHub and LinkedIn. These are operated by third parties under their own terms and privacy practices, for which no responsibility is accepted. No content is loaded from them unless you choose to follow a link.
